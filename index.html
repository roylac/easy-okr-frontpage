Features

Everything you need to focus on what matters

From goal-setting to celebration, EasyOKR has all the tools modern teams need — without the complexity.

Goal Setting & Alignment

Create company, team, and individual OKRs in minutes. Cascade goals down your org chart so every employee sees how their work connects to the big picture.

Real-time Progress Tracking

Live dashboards show exactly where every objective stands. Colour-coded health indicators let you spot at-risk goals before they go off track.

Team & Individual OKRs

Support cross-functional collaboration with shared OKRs, while also giving individuals ownership of personal key results that ladder up to team outcomes.

Check-ins & Status Updates

Weekly nudges keep teams accountable. Structured check-ins capture blockers, confidence levels, and momentum — all in one place, not buried in email chains.

Analytics & Reporting

Executive-level reports generated in a click. Track OKR completion rates, identify patterns, and share progress with stakeholders using beautiful, shareable dashboards.

Integrations

Connects with Slack, Microsoft Teams, Jira, Notion, and 40+ tools your team already uses. No more context-switching — updates flow where work happens.

Customisable Review Cycles

Quarterly, monthly, or rolling — you choose the rhythm. Set automated review milestones, scoring sessions, and retrospectives that match how your organisation works.

Role-based Access Control

Fine-grained permissions ensure the right people see the right goals. Keep sensitive company objectives private while keeping team-level OKRs fully transparent.
